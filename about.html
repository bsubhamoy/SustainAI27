Conference Overview

A meeting point for
AI and sustainability.

Sust@inAI 27 is the International Conference on Sustainable Artificial Intelligence and Green Computing, taking place in Sydney from April 20–22, 2027.

The question behind the conference

How do we advance artificial intelligence without accelerating its environmental cost?

Sust@inAI 27 brings the AI and sustainable-computing communities together to explore technical, infrastructural, environmental and governance solutions.

Focus

From efficient models to verifiable sustainability

We welcome original research, applied work, systems studies, interdisciplinary perspectives and policy contributions across the six tracks.
